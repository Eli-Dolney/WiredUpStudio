NET & TAME / SAFARI PACK

Created: October 3, 2026
Original models: Elephant, Giraffe, Lion, Zebra, Capybara, Meerkat, Red Panda, Penguin.
The eight species match the existing Wildlife Reserve roster.
Direction: faceted low-poly forms, natural proportions, restrained faces.
The supplied Sketchfab collection was viewed as a style reference only.
No seller geometry, textures, downloads or purchased assets were used.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- .bbmodel: editable polygon meshes with embedded color palette in Blockbench.
- .obj + .mtl: static mesh/material handoff for Blender or a 3D importer.
- .rig.json: motion-group names and pivot positions matching the game's conventions.
- manifest.json: pack contents and measured triangle/vertex counts.
- Safari-Pack.svg/png: geometry-rendered front-three-quarter and side views.
- 01-Safari: complete editable pack; 01-Safari-Foundations preserves the approved trio.
- 02-Safari-Rigged: eight weighted glTF skins, skeletal clips and pose evidence.

UNITS AND EDITING
OBJ and pivot JSON use studs, Y-up, facing -Z. Blockbench uses four units per stud.
Quadrupeds separate body, head, tail and four legs; the tailless capybara has six
groups. Meerkat arms and penguin flippers use their own groups. All groups include
pivot metadata for the next rigging pass.
The 01-Safari static sources are not skinned. The separate 02-Safari-Rigged exports
add weighted leg/knee/foot bones, a giraffe neck and seven skeletal clips.
Each motion group is now one connected, closed shell, with internal intersections
removed. Adjacent groups overlap at their anatomical joints for later rigid-part
animation; this is not one continuous, skin-deformation-ready surface. Do not run
the older cube-only import-blockbench.mjs on these polygon models.

POLISH PASS
Leg roots taper inside the torso; facial details are seated against the skin.
Penguin/meerkat bellies and red-panda coat markings are surface colors, not raised
slabs. Tusks, tail tufts, ankles and other formerly detached parts are connected.
The preview uses per-pixel depth instead of painter sorting, removing false cracks.
Asset checks verify each group and the whole assembled animal are connected,
alongside closed topology, winding, palette UVs and matching OBJ exports.
The polished Penguin was visually checked in Blockbench. The inspection sheet
provides front, left, rear and right views of all eight exported models.

GAME STATUS
NetAndTameZoo-LivingHabitats.rbxlx adds procedural native-Part fallback animation
and habitat activities. The polished mesh skins still need owner import in Studio;
they are not embedded or uploaded by the file generator. See README's Animated
Safari Rigs steps and tools/InstallSafariRig.lua. Mesh templates automatically
replace the corresponding native-Part model once installed. Check owner access,
scale, catching and performance before publishing. No animation asset IDs are
fabricated; procedural Bone/Motor6D playback uses the shared numeric pose source.
Current glTF heights are game-scale studs (Elephant 12, Giraffe 15). Older static
Blockbench/OBJ sources keep their approved modeling scale.
The giraffe's coat is modeled into the surface rather than a painted texture;
it uses more triangles and is a candidate for texture baking in an optimization pass.

OCEAN PACK / OCTOBER 4, 2026
The Ocean models use the same original faceted style and surface-color approach.
Targets: crab width 4.5 studs; seal length 6.5; turtle length 5.5; otter length 5;
dolphin length 9; manta width 10; octopus span 6; shark length 9.5.
Crab +X is scuttle direction; all models face -Z. Water-reference height is recorded
for swimmers; the otter's waterline passes through its back for surface floating.
Grounded animals are crab, seal and octopus. Swimming poses may extend below Y=0;
their origin is a swimming reference, not a seabed contact point.
Each rig has Idle, Walk, Flee, Rest, Eat, Drink and Alert. Walk maps to the species'
locomotion. Drink is a compatible interaction for aquatic anatomy. Marine clips
come from AnimalMotion.lua and the glTF viewer plays the actual exported tracks.
The octopus has eight three-joint arm chains; the crab has eight articulated legs,
two wrist chains and moving pincers. Manta wing chains, dolphin/shark tail chains,
otter spine/paws, and seal/turtle flippers retain their own motion profiles.

Ocean-Pack.png: geometry lineup; Ocean-Inspection.png: six views per animal.
04-Ocean-Rigged/Ocean-Rig-Poses.png: sampled skinned poses.
Ocean-Animation-Viewer.html: self-contained offline WebGL glTF player with all
eight animals, seven clips, camera controls, pause and timeline scrubbing.
Open .bbmodel files in Blockbench for geometry edits or glTF in a skin-capable
editor for weighted rig edits. OBJ is static and does not preserve animation.
Use --pack ocean on the model, rig, render and art/rig test commands. These
commands never regenerate Safari assets. tools/build-ocean-viewer.mjs refreshes
the player after changing exported Ocean rigs. No place build or upload is made.
Studio import: Custom rig, Keep Zero Influence Bones ON, Ignore Vertex Colors OFF.
Use tools/InstallAnimalRig.lua after the latest scripts/metadata are synchronized.
The new water species still need future water routing and habitat integration.

ARCTIC PACK / OCTOBER 4, 2026
Eight original winter models, 25,652 triangles total; each below 10,000 triangles.
Targets in studs: ArcticFox height 3.5, SnowyOwl height 3.5, PolarBear height 7,
Walrus length 9, ArcticHare height 3.3, MuskOx height 6.5, Reindeer height 8.5
including antlers, HarpSeal length 6.5. All face -Z and use Y=0 ground reference.
The owl uses grounded waddle/wing-hop clips; this pass adds no flight system.
Each has Idle, Walk, Flee, Rest, Eat, Drink and Alert, exported from AnimalMotion.lua.
Leg/knee/foot chains, fox tail, articulated owl wings and hare ears, and separate
seal/walrus flippers provide species-specific movement. Markings use face colors,
not floating decals. Tapered limb roots remain embedded through folded poses.
